FG Backup: privacy policy

FG Backup is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer.

With the owner's permission, it uses Google's "drive.file" access to create, read and delete only the files it makes itself in the owner's own Google Drive: encrypted copies of the owner's work files. It cannot see any other file in that Drive.

It collects no personal information, keeps no data about anyone, and shares nothing with anyone. It sends data nowhere except the owner's own Google Drive.
